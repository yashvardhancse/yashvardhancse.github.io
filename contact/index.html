What kind of work do you take on?

Freelance work, technical guidance and collaboration in six areas: software development (web applications, mobile apps, backend systems), low latency trading infrastructure, embedded systems, project discussion (technical consulting and architecture planning), guidance on open source contributions, and UI UX design.

Yes, it is one of the six areas: high-performance systems and algorithms. I work as a Quantitative Developer, Researcher & Trader at a Dubai & Singapore based trading firm. I work in C, C++, Java, Python and Rust, plus JavaScript and TypeScript through Node.js and Next.js. For web and mobile: FastAPI, Django, Node.js, Next.js, Flutter, Streamlit, JavaFX and SQL.
