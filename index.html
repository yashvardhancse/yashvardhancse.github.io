01About

Systems thinker, long-distance rider.

I’m a Computer Science student and a quantitative developer. I write code for markets: backtesters, scanners and the plumbing around them.

I’m drawn to the hard parts of software: low-latency design, market microstructure, CUDA and distributed systems. Right now I’m studying market-making models (Avellaneda-Stoikov and Cartea-Jaimungal) and writing the notes up on the blog.

Away from the screen I ride long distances. I started Manipal Riders Club, now 100 members strong, and I photograph the trips. Planning a route, packing for surprises and keeping a calm head turn out to be good engineering habits too.
